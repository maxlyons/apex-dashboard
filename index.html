PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy describes how Apex Dashboard ("the App") handles your personal data. Apex Dashboard is a personal productivity tool used to track habits, goals, and health metrics.

DATA WE ACCESS

When you connect your WHOOP account, the App requests read-only access to your WHOOP health data, including strain, recovery, heart rate variability (HRV), and sleep metrics. This data is used solely to display your personal metrics within the App.

HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Your WHOOP data is used exclusively to populate your personal dashboard. It is not sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. All data is processed locally in your browser or stored in your personal account.

DATA STORAGE

Habit, goal, and journal data is stored locally in your browser or in your personal database. WHOOP access tokens are stored securely and used only to fetch your personal health data. No data is stored on shared servers.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App integrates with WHOOP via their official API. Your use of WHOOP's services is governed by WHOOP's own Privacy Policy. The App also uses the Anthropic API to power the AI mentor feature. Conversation data sent to Anthropic is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

DATA SHARING

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data is yours.

YOUR RIGHTS

You may disconnect your WHOOP account or delete your data at any time by clearing your browser storage or revoking access through the WHOOP app settings.

CONTACT

For any questions about this policy, contact: apex@apexstoicism.com
