01 — OVERVIEW

Apex Dashboard is a personal performance OS for athletes and high-performers. WHOOP data is the foundation of the health layer — giving users a real-time picture of their body's readiness alongside their habits, goals, and journal. The integration is read-only, personal use only, and data is never shared with third parties.
